How the system works

Each tank is a fixed cone-roof crude tank that receives stabilised crude, stores it warm enough to stay pumpable through the Turkmen winter, and sends it to export. A fuel gas blanket keeps air out of the vapour space, and vapours displaced during filling go to the vapour recovery unit rather than to air.

Crude receipt

Crude arrives on the 16" tank receipt header from the degassing boot (16"-PR-B013-038-H60) and from the metering skids X-008/009/010 (18"-PL-B013-4229-W, reduced 18"×16", tie-in TP-026). A 16" riser to each tank reduces 16"×10" and enters shell nozzle N1 through inlet shutdown valve SDV-10x-1.

Export & dewatering

Crude leaves through N2 (16") and outlet shutdown valve SDV-10x-2, expands to 20" and splits at a 20" manifold into three valved drops: pump suction header A (to export pumps via 16"-PR-B013-208-H60), pump suction header B (16"-PR-B013-037-H60) and, through 20"×12"×6" reducers, the 6" dewatering header to pumps CPSF-P-127-1 & 2. N3 (6") draws bottom water into the same outlet line; N4 (3") drains to the sewage system.

Recirculation & inter-tank transfer

The 10" header is fed from the inter-tank transfer pumps P-013A/B/C (10"-PL-B013-4209-WE, TP-004) and from the export pumps (10"-PR-B013-049-H60). A 10" riser returns crude to each tank through N6. Transfer is limited to 495 m³/h (Note 9), and it may not run at the same time as filling from the degassing boot because the VRU is sized only for 500 m³/h of inlet (Note 10).

Fuel gas blanketing

Fuel gas at 31 °C (6"-FG) passes through PV-4147/4148/4149 (fail closed) and enters the roof at N21 as 10"-FG. PIC-41xx, fed by roof transmitter PIT-41xx on N22, opens the valve when vapour-space pressure falls below 1" WG (Note 1), for example while pumping out or during cooling. Each PV has an 8" globe bypass (GLV, normally closed) and isolation valves. PZT/PZI-41xx gives an independent pressure reading.

Vapour recovery & overpressure protection

During filling, displaced vapour leaves N24 through a locked-open 20" triple-eccentric BFV into 20"-PG-B013-4133/4134/4135-W to VRU CPSF-23-X-011 A/B. PIT-4156/4157/4158 monitors each line and signals the VRU (PY-4203A/B). If the VRU cannot keep up, or the tank pulls a vacuum, four PVRVs per tank (three duty locked open, one spare locked closed) relieve at +7.23" WG or admit air at −0.88" WG through 12" vents to a safe location, each with an integral flame arrester.

Hot oil heating

Hot oil at 170 °C (3"-HO) flows through ESDV-41xx and the coil (N8A/B in, N7A/B out) and returns at about 100 °C through on/off gap-action valve XV-41xx and restriction orifice RO-41xx to the common return header (Note 6). TIT-4135/4136/4137, just below the low-low liquid level (Note 12), opens XV below 35 °C and closes it above 40 °C. TIT-4141/4142/4143 alarms the return at 115/90 °C.

Safeguarding

  • High-high oil temperature: SIL 2 TIT-4175/4176/4177 (N26) trips ESD-103 at 45 °C and closes hot oil ESDV (Notes 4, 11, 16).
  • Low-low level: LSLL-10x closes the hot oil inlet valve so the coil is never heated uncovered (Note 2).
  • High-high level: LSHH-10x alarms LA HH to stop overfilling.
  • SDV-10x-1/-2 isolate the tank inlet and outlet. HS-10x-1/2 give open/close at the DCS and at local push-buttons outside the bund wall (Note 3).
  • Hot oil ESDVs are fail-closed and tight shut-off. XSOV vents the actuator to atmosphere; XZSO/XZSC confirm the valve position.

Drawing notes

General notes

  1. For legends & symbols refer to DOTL standard drawings STDS-PR-PID-001 to 005.
  2. New instruments and manual valves tag numbers shall be prefixed with CPSF-23.

Notes

  1. Fuel gas control valve shall be opened in case tank pressure falls below 1" WG.
  2. Tank low-low oil level shall close hot oil inlet SDV.
  3. Local push buttons (open/close) shall be provided outside the bund wall.
  4. High-high oil temperature shall close hot oil inlet SDV.
  5. On-off gap action control valve.
  6. Hot oil return lines from each tank shall be connected to the common hot oil return header in the nearest location.
  7. Alteration to the existing tanks shall be certified by the EPIC contractor.
  8. Winterization insulation shall be provided.
  9. During inter-tank transfer operations, max flow rate shall be limited to 495 m³/h for 10" line.
  10. Since the VRU (CPSF-23-X-011 A/B) capacity is based on the crude oil filling rate, 75,000 BOPD (max 500 m³/h crude inlet to tank), simultaneous operation of tank filling (from degassing boot to the existing tanks) and inter-tank transfer operation (from new tanks to the existing tanks) is not possible because of the VRU capacity limitation.
  11. High-high oil temperature shall close the inlet hot oil ESDV.
  12. Location of temperature instrument shall be just below LLLL of oil.
  13. Tight shut-off triple eccentric.
  14. Vent at safe location.
  15. Flame arrestor shall be integral part of PVRV.
  16. SIL level of CPSF-23-TIT-4175/4176/4177 is "2".
  17. Existing coils will be checked for the mechanical integrity per the hot oil conditions.

Reading notes

  • This is a schematic redraw for review and HAZOP use, not a replacement for the controlled drawing. Small hand valves, vents/drains (BV-47xx, BV-51xx, ¾" NC) and instrument-air branches to each SDV are listed in tooltips or omitted for clarity.
  • The PVRV setting is printed as "−088" WG / 7.23" WG"; it is read here as −0.88" WG vacuum / +7.23" WG pressure.
  • The insulation suffixes (H, W, WE, H60) are not defined on this sheet. W is probably winterization insulation (Note 8) and H probably hot insulation, but confirm both against STDS-PR-PID-001.
  • The "MOU-40 108-1" bubble near each fuel gas line, and the internal routing of the three-way 20" outlet manifold valves (152-59 / 152-66), are not legible enough to pin down. They are shown as on the original without interpretation.
  • Signal lines from PI-4156/4157/4158 to PY-4203A/B (P&ID 0669-29-03) are listed in the tag schedule and not drawn.
