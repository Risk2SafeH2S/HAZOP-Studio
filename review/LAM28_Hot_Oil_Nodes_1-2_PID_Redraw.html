How it works

Until now the hot oil unit flushed wells with diesel. In this change, well crude that has already been separated is filtered and stabilised in Bullet Tank-C. A new feed pump then sends it through the existing hot oil heater to the wells. Part of the heated crude is returned to the tank, and the tank vents to a new cold vent instead of to flare.

Node 1 — crude to Bullet Tank-C

V-100 / V-301 oil→TP-01A/B · TP-04→3" lines 8086-H / 8083-H→ST-017 A/B→8085-H→TP-06A/B · BV-352→V-312 N1

Crude comes from either the test separator V-100 (TP-01A/B, 3" 600#) or the Repco separator V-301 (TP-04, 3" 300# downstream of LV-303). The two lines join and pass through the new basket strainer ST-017 A/B, which has a locked-closed 100% bypass, then enter Bullet Tank-C at N1 through the existing 6" BV-352. The separators push the flow on their own pressure, so there is no pump in this node. At each spec break (D013/C013 → B013) the line drops to the B013 150# class. Before start-up, the fluid is checked for solids, gas and water (030 Note 4).

When V-100 is used, its free water drains separately through TP-02A/B and a check valve to TP-03 and the LLP header (030 Note 3).

Node 2 — feed pump and heater loop

V-312 N4→TP-07→P-003→8093-H / 8048-H→TP-10A/B→H-001→TP-11A/B→8097-H→TP-05 → V-312 N6

P-003 draws crude from N4 (the end-head nitrogen connection, elevation not shown) and pumps it into the existing 3" diesel supply line at TP-10A/B. The diesel isolation stays closed during this service. From there the crude passes through the hose and BV-001 to heater H-001, which heats it to 75–80 °C at most. The HP PD booster P-002 sends hot crude to the wells. A 2" line from the heater outlet (TP-11A/B) returns heated oil to V-312 N6 so the tank contents warm up and stabilise (062 Note 4). A 2" minimum-flow line (8094-H) protects P-003 when its discharge is throttled.

Venting and stabilisation

For stabilisation, the V-312 lines to LAM-16 flare are closed (Note 5), TP-09 isolates the fuel gas conditioning skid line, and the tank vents through N10 (TP-08), 2"-VF-B013-8095-X, to a new cold vent about 3 m high at a safe location. The V-001 vent/drain drum vent joins the same stack (TP-12A/B, 2"-VF-B013-8096-X). The stack height is on HOLD 1 until the dispersion calculation is done.

Protection on this scope

  • LSHH-1004 → PSD-232 at 2200 mm protects V-312 against overfill from the separators and from heated-oil recirculation.
  • LSLL-1004 → PSD-231 at 300 mm protects P-003 against loss of suction.
  • P-003 has a check valve on the discharge and a minimum-flow line. It is started and stopped locally from the LCP.
  • TI-527 on the heated-oil return confirms the 75–80 °C limit. A heater trip or control on this limit is not shown on these sheets.
  • Pressure on every new branch is shown by a DBB-isolated local gauge (PI-543 … PI-551). There are no new transmitters.

Drawing notes

030 · TDE Test Separator V-100

  • Well selection for stabilized crude deployment to replace the diesel flushing shall be free of sand, min gas and oil.
  • Free water drain line to LLP header during hot oil flushing operation.
  • Fluid shall be analysed in LAM-28 to ensure that the fluid is free from solid, min. gas & water before starting hot oil operation.
  • Test separator shall be operated close to the wellhead choke valve downstream pressure.
  • Well 192B is selected for this trial operation.
  • Test separator shall be operated for hot oil flushing, if free water separation is required.

031 · Repco Separator V-301

  • Repco separator shall be dedicated for hot oil unit operation, during heating of stabilized crude oil from flushing operation.
  • Basket strainer is available in stock.

041 · Bullet Tank-C V-312

  • Min recirculation line for pump protection.
  • Bullet tank shall be dedicated for hot oil operation during flushing operation with crude oil.
  • Vent to atm shall be operated by closing the line to LAM-16 flare for oil stabilization.
  • Pump is available in stock.
  • NC (normally close) is applicable when bullet tank is used for hot oil operation.
  • HOLD 1: new cold vent stack height based on dispersion calculation.

062 · Hot Oil Units

  • Well fluid shall flow from test separator or Repco separator to the bullet tank after filtration in basket strainer. Stabilized oil shall be pumped to the hot oil unit through booster pumps.
  • Heater oil for recirculation to bullet tank.
  • Oil shall be heated to maximum temperature of 75 °C–80 °C to avoid excess flushing of gas.

025 · Vent & Drain Drum V-001

  • HOLD 1: dispersion calculation (new cold vent).

Reading notes

  • This is a schematic of the new scope for review and HAZOP use, not a controlled drawing. Existing plant that does not touch the new lines (well manifolds, LP headers, V-001 inlet headers and so on) is left out.
  • The mark-up places the V-312 vent line (TP-08) in Node 2. The repo's Rev B HAZOP worksheet put the V-312 vessel, its instruments and the atmospheric vent in Node 1. Agree which convention applies before the next session.
  • The pump is tagged L028-21-P-003 on the pump symbol but L028-68-P-003 in the data box and on sheet 062.
  • On sheet 041, line 8097-H is labelled "from HOU booster pump L028-68-P-001". On sheet 062 the same line leaves the heater H-001 outlet at TP-11A/B. The redraw follows sheet 062.
  • Line number 3"-PR-B013-8087-H appears both on the strainer bypass (sheet 031) and next to the free-water tie-in on sheet 030. The free-water line has no number of its own.
  • Several items are still undefined: PI-54X, PI-XXX, BV-XXX valve numbers, the P-003 driver power ("XX kW") and the 3"-DF-B013-XXX-X diesel line.
