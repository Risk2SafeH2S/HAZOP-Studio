How it works

Separation

Well fluid from Zhdanov-25 enters each horizontal 2-phase separator through a 12" 600# isolation valve (BV-007 on H-07, BV-008 on H-08) at nozzle N2. Gas leaves from N1 on top. Oil leaves from N5 at the bottom, where the piping class drops from C013 to B013 (Class 150). A 12" crossover (021-X, valves 21-GV-280, 21-GV-134 and 21-GV-269) joins H-07 N3 to H-08 N3.

Oil to multiphase pumps

Each separator's oil passes through a duplex strainer: FT-111A/B for H-07 and S-002A/B for H-08. It then goes to the multiphase pumps on 8"-017-H60 and 6"-018-H60. BV-114 cross-ties the H-08 oil line into the H-07 strainer header, so either strainer set can serve either separator.

Gas manifold and scrubbing

Gas from both separators joins the 14" header (034-X) at the outlet manifold, through 21-GV-216 and 21-GV-120. From the header it goes to scrubber V-01 (BV-218) and/or V-04 (BV-219), or around both on the bypass (BV-217). Branches off the separator gas lines feed the old manifold (21-BV-215) and the multiphase pumps (21-BV-205 / 206, 3").

Metering and export

Scrubbed gas passes meter FE-001 / 21-FQIR-001, which has bypass BV-227. It then splits to:

  • the emergency flare, 12" via BV-238 / BV-233
  • the chemical plant, 6" via BV-230 / BV-239
  • the NGDU, 4" via BV-447
  • the carbon plant, 20"-054-X via GLV-001, together with the 20" line from the old manifold

Condensate return

Liquid knocked out in V-01 and V-04 drains on 4"-031-X (BV-247, BV-224). It goes either through air-driven pump P-001 or straight through bypass 21-GV-148 when scrubber pressure is enough. It then passes check valve 21-FSV-141 back to N4 of both separators (BV-089 on H-07, 21-BV-155 on H-08). P-001 runs on air from receiver V-252, which is charged by the 18.5 kW compressor.

Overpressure protection

PSV-224 and PSV-255 (14 barg) protect the Class 150 outlet manifold. PSV-102, PSV-102-B and PSV-102-D (6.2 barg) protect the scrubber section. All five discharge into one 6" flare line that runs to the emergency flare through BV-234. The separators themselves show only PSHH-222 / 223 and level switches on these sheets. No PSV or shutdown valve is drawn for them.

Reading notes

  • This is a schematic redraw for review and HAZOP use, not a controlled drawing. Small vents, drains and sample points are summarised, not all drawn.
  • Relief mismatch to check: the scrubbers' relief valves are set at 6.2 barg, but the manifold PSVs that feed the same header are set at 14 barg. In normal operation the header is open straight to the scrubbers, so the 6.2 barg valves (PSV-102 on the inlet header, PSV-102-B and PSV-102-D on the vessels) protect them. If the scrubbers can be isolated while the manifold stays live, confirm that the 14 barg setting doesn't leave the scrubber inlet piping exposed above 6.2 barg.
  • Separator protection not shown: H-07 and H-08 (6.4 MPa) have no PSV, ESDV or SDV on these sheets. PSHH-222 / 223, LSH and LSL are shown, but nothing shows what they act on. Their relief may be on another sheet. Confirm it.
  • Tag issues:
    • LG-175 appears twice on H-08.
    • PSV-255 on sheet 005 is PSV-225 on sheet 013.
    • Several tags are still placeholders: LIT-XXX, LG-XXX, PSH-XXX, PSL-XXX, 6"-PR-B013-XXX-X, and air compressor "XXX".
    • V-04 and the V-01 capacity / operating pressure fields are blank.
  • Data box units: the separator nominal pressure is printed "6.4 MPa (kgf/m3)", and the compressor dimension unit is "sm". Both are transcribed as printed.
  • Interpretations: the direction of the H-07 to H-08 N3 crossover is taken from the arrows on sheet 003. The P-001 air connection and exhaust are simplified.
